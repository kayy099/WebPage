Dear Class of 2025

Date: October 6, 2025

I know it is hard to believe, but somehow, in the blink of an eye, we have reached the finish line. What once seemed a daunting herculean task now feels like a magical fairytale that ended too soon. Only now do we realize how precious those moments were, as we step into a world brimming with talent as fresh novices. But we are all prepared! These years have not only instilled in us the confidence to tackle challenges that go against the grain but also shaped us into individuals capable of breaking down complex problems into simple, solvable parts. The rigorous curriculum and supercharged ecosystem have unknowingly transformed us all from wide-eyed, wet-behind-the-ear teenagers into competent, curious, quick-witted engineers.

As I try to condense these four years of joy, filled with gales of laughter and indelible memories, I cross my heart, they are beyond what words can express. It feels like yesterday that I collected my induction kit and my parents showed me around campus, the same campus I will tour my friends and family as my alma mater!

This rollercoaster was set in motion during the bleak times of the pandemic. With my dad co-attending most of Rajiv Sir’s online lectures and me somehow acing the course thanks to his meticulous notes, the journey had its own unique start. What followed was a series of cramped and chaotic semesters to get our batch back on track. The transition to offline classes and meeting all those online characters in person was a completely different experience. The masks, digital screens, and otherwise were slowly fading, and the camaraderie we shared virtually became real. And as they say, nothing creates a stronger bond than being stuck in the same trench. Voila, in just a few weeks time, we became chaddi buddies from total strangers!

With academics and assignments setting the tune and rhythm, the dance of college life unfolded on the flip side. The institute gave us the liberty to grow, explore, and develop holistically. More importantly, it taught us the importance of time. Whether it was balancing heavy course projects with the urge to watch World Cup finals, or hosting multiple Padma Awardees while selecting electives. Setting up a new chapter, or closing a sponsor deal while juggling a full semester load and demanding positions of responsibility, IIIT Delhi offered us a simulation of real life. Even while soaking in the euphoria and magic of fests, pearls of wisdom that would last a lifetime were always around. Interactions with our seniors and passing on a remixed version of their gyan ki baatein to our juniors enriched the journey and fostered a sense of brotherhood. Insti set the stage and served as a launchpad for us to hone our soft skills and aim for the stars.

Throughout this journey, it was our teachers who stood right besides us as guides and role models. The ones we always looked up to, who taught us to think on our own feet and ignited a spark in us. Lately, the ones with whom we share and vent our thoughts while learning from their problem-solving finesse and life experiences :)
Equally important were our supportive staff who worked quietly behind the scenes and our dear parents and siblings, who were our silent anchors, always cheering us on and rooting for us at every step.

Among innumerable learnings over the past few years, the lessons of ethics, professionalism, and the strong ethos that IIIT Delhi stands for are the ones I will always carry forward. I will cherish the friendships I have built, miss the carefree campus life, and treasure the crazy experiences we have had. I will smile at the silly gossips we shared and laugh at the 11:59 deadlines that once felt like the end of the world :P
I will remember all the fun we had, every error we debugged, and every little adventure we shared. I would relive all these years in a heartbeat!

It is with bittersweet feelings that I turn this in at 11:59 PM, reminiscing and recollecting countless moments of nostalgia. My first fumble on stage, those unforgettable electronics labs, nightmares during algorithms paper or my first class as a teaching assistant. Those insightful weekly meetings with Sujay Sir and Sumit Sir, late-night Maggi cravings, chai-coffee dates with caffeine-dependent lifeforms, long chess matches in the library, poster presentations, interview preparations, and all the demos, quizzes, midsems, and endsems that marked our journey. I don’t know how to fit them all here. Perhaps they can only be stored in my default memory space up in my brain!

Amidst all uncertainty and with the thought of whether we will ever meet again lingering in our minds, we must realise change has always been our constant companion, and our batch has always embraced it. We attended classes online during the lockdown, switched to offline and later hybrid sessions during periods of high air pollution, and then adapted to a six-month internship policy. We faced every challenge together. I am sure we will do well in the diverse avenues we choose to explore. We have been trained to approach any pursuit from first principles and, more importantly, together. I hope that our paths cross again, as they always did in the hallways of our sweet little campus. Govindpuri se left will always lead to a special place in our hearts.

Induction'21: A week to remember!

Date: January 4, 2022

After two years of preparation, planning, practice, and postponements, the JEE journey was finally over. I could finally enjoy some holidays without any I-should-be-studying guilt pricking my conscience. With the results declared, the hype, analysis, discussions, and doubts; all started again. Which institute to choose? Which branch to prefer? To go with the JEE Mains rank or the JEE Advanced rank? And so many more. With the guidance of some well-acquainted veterans in the field of academia, we managed to settle the dust. IIIT Delhi was the best option: given the vast experience and high qualification of its faculty, infrastructure, research, and coding culture among undergraduate students. In fact, I still remember the excitement gushing in me when IIITD was finalized!

On the 24th of December, Santa did send me a gift. An email dropped in my inbox asking me to collect my 'Induction Kit' from the Office of Student Affairs the next day. Although it was not my first visit to IIITD, it was certainly my first as a IIITDian, making the visit all the more fascinating. After collecting my kit, I explored the beautiful campus surroundings with my family.

The first day of Induction began with over 600+ students eagerly anticipating the commencement of the next phase of their life. We were all randomly divided into groups, each with two or three mentors to help us adapt to college life and make our transition from school to college life more fluid.

I was in group 12 with Vaibhav bhaiya and Ishita didi as our mentors. Few rounds of skribbl, and the ice was broken! Our mentors gave us an overview of their student life (both online and offline) and shared some of their gyaan with us!

We were then given a virtual tour of the lovely campus. Post lunch, we learned about all the clubs, the student council, the cultural council, and the student senate at IIITD and later enjoyed many poems, dialogues, monologues, and songs performed by my batch mates in the Open Mic Session conducted by LitSoc-the literature club.

The evening was filled with laughter as we played Mafia Night and enjoyed repartee with the seniors who shared funny anecdotes of their hostel and campus life. We even loved the performance by Rajat Sood, the well-known pomedian (poetry + comedy) who kept everyone laughing until the very end.

Day two started with a session on the college's social media presence and a welcome address by our Director Prof. Ranjan Bose. We were then greeted by the Associate Dean of Student Affairs, Dean of Academic Affairs, Dean of Innovation Research and the Development and Associate Dean of Communication and Alumni Affairs. This day we got to attend the keynote talks by Mr. Saurabh Jain (Founder Fun2Do labs, Ex-Vice President Paytm) and Mr. Manish Gulati (Engineering Manager Google, Founder Shiksha.com) who shared various hurdles and milestones they encountered in their journey to success.

Later most of us enjoyed the BGMI Battle Royale tournament organised by the Sports Council. But let me tell you the Cultural Council's 'Beg, Borrow and Steal' event did steal the show! We were given a list of fun, odd, and out-of-the-box tasks to complete in a limited time.

We created memories as we licked the cream off a full pack of biscuits, clicking a selfie with 3 dogs of different colors, clicked a selfie with the patli pin wala Nokia charger, did the Vrikshasana, sang a duet with our mentor and so much more! The day came to an end with students letting their talent shine in the Karaoke Night organized by Audiobytes- the music club. I played a little tune on the flute and thoroughly enjoyed the performances by my batchmates.

The following day we had official meetings lined up with the Registrar Office, Office of Student Affairs, Alumni and Communication office and the academic office enumerating more about the institute and its regulations. We then had an interactive session with Ms. Pragya Vats (Head of Campaigns, Save the Children). The afternoon saw us witness sessions on the research culture, hostel culture and start-up culture in the institute. Freshers also had an opportunity to connect with various clubs such as Tasveer, Êvariste, GirlUp Udaan, Designhub, Leanin, Enactus, Salt n' Pepper, Biobytes, Micdrop, Astronuts, Trivialis, Women in Tech, E-cell, Meraki, Madtoes and Machan through the amazing events organized by them. Events like the privilege walk, joke writing, astronomy quiz, induction quiz, recreating memes, online-hunt, devil's advocate, TechBazaar and ideathon kept us engaged and gave us the chance to create new friends.

I got to play chess after a really long time and absolutely loved the chess tournament conducted by the 65th Square. Day three came to a halt with the mesmerising voice of Mr. Zaman Khan's virtual performance. To add a tinge of fun and entertainment, the institute invited the very witty and energetic anchor GD Sanyal. They really did manage to recreate the joy and magic of a live show!

The fourth day started with an interactive buddy talk with our alumni association. It made us feel proud of our seniors who pursued and excelled in every field imaginable. The institute hosted Dr. Lipika Dey (Principal Scientist, TCS Research) as a keynote speaker. She threw light on various opportunities we as IIITDians have in future. Inhouse sessions detailing the IT infrastructure, finance, entrepreneurship and facility management services were also conducted. Later everyone enjoyed the informative sessions by FooBar, the competitive programming club, and d4rkc0de, the Cyber Security Club. An interactive session was planned for the parents and all their doubts were resolved patiently by the faculty and staff. Later in the day, Finnexia- the finance club of IIITD conducted Mock Stock. 'Market mein sabse bada jhokim, jhokim na lene mein hai. Ek din mein itna tho Mock Stock ne sikha diya.' Dialogues apart, all of us really enjoyed this interesting and informative contest.

This was followed by an evening extravaganza where all of us hummed along with the melodious voice of Ms. Pallavi Baberwal and rolled on the floor laughing to the jokes of Mr. Sumit Sourav!

We were all already getting nostalgic on the final day of Induction 2021. We started with the briefing of the innovative apps developed by the office of students affairs for the ease of the student community. Later we also learnt about the many facilities that the institute’s library offers. We were also taught the significance of the health and wellbeing centre and informed about the student mentorship programme. The session by Prof. Pankaj Jalote, Founding Director of IIIT Delhi was the one which I'm sure every single one of us thoroughly enjoyed listening to. He told us how to make the most of the time spent at IIITD and inspired us to create four years of amazing memories!

Soon after, departmental sessions were organised to formally welcome their respective incoming students. We got to know the important details of the courses, methodology, teaching pedagogy, and projects. Towards the evening we were introduced to Cyborg, Byld, and the Electroholics clubs. We learnt the basics of Arduino, robotics and software development. But the day was not over yet! It was time for day 2 of the much awaited Talent Night! So many kids, so many talents, this event gave us a chance to celebrate and rejoice in the diversity at IIITD!

Induction 2021 was indeed a once-in-a-lifetime event for all of us! And for that, we are forever grateful to all our faculty, staff, and all our awesome seniors who must have worked so hard behind the scenes, despite their busy schedules!
Though we were all separated by miles, we saw new bonds and new friendships emerge. Let me end by saying that in the cookie of IIITD life, Induction’21 was a chocolate chip! :P
